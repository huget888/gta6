Solmar Nights

An open-world crime story

You are Jax Marlow, a wheelman fresh out of the Solmar docks with empty pockets and a fast reputation. Rico has jobs. Mara has scores. The Solmar PD has your name on a list. Take the jobs, take the cars, take the city — and don't get caught.

WASD / arrows — move & drive  •  E — steal / enter car
Mouse — aim  •  Click — shoot  •  F — punch
P — pause  •  H — save  •  M — mute
An original game. Solmar City, Jax Marlow, Rico and Mara are fictional — no real city, person or game is copied.
